Spare parts, kept original.

A complete reference of authentic Techimpex components — burners, thermocouples, gas valves, grids and ignition assemblies — engineered, kept in stock and shipped from Conegliano. Every part is original equipment, designed to preserve the longevity and certification of the appliance it serves.

How to order
Identify the part you need by browsing or searching the catalogue below. Each entry includes a reference code and, where available, an original photograph of the component. To place a request, write to info@marinecookers.com quoting the reference code, the photograph (or its link) and the model of your cooker. Our office will reply with availability, lead time and shipping arrangements.
